## Observations

## Assumptions

One-off costs are spread evenly across the stated year. Run-rate savings start in month 7 of the stated year and continue to month 36. Payback is the month after which the cumulative net position stays at or above zero. Savings factors: high 0.9, medium 0.6, low 0.3. Cost uplifts (also applied to negative run-rate items, which are extra recurring costs): high 1, medium 1.15, low 1.3. These factors are illustrative assumptions, not benchmarks: set your own.
