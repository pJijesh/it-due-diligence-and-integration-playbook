# Day 1 checklist

A practical checklist for the first day of ownership. Tick items as they are confirmed, and record who confirmed them. Day 1 is about continuity and control. Transformation comes later.

> Scope. IT only. Employment, contract, tax and regulatory steps on Day 1 are led by the relevant advisers. This checklist is not legal advice.

The matching tasks are in [`data/integration-plan.csv`](../data/integration-plan.csv), in the Pre-close and Day 1 phases.

## A. Before completion (readiness)

- [ ] Day 1 requirements list agreed with the target (payroll, orders, warehouse, invoicing, banking, email, phones, website).
- [ ] Completion date checked against payroll cut-off, year end, audit dates and peak trading.
- [ ] Integration lead and steering group named, with contact details.
- [ ] Who holds administrator credentials at completion agreed, and break-glass access prepared.
- [ ] Minimum security conditions for connecting the target agreed, and the target kept separate until it meets them.
- [ ] List of contracts and licences needing consent or notice on completion, with owners.
- [ ] Transitional services schedule agreed for anything the seller or a related party will keep providing, with exit dates.
- [ ] Support route and contact numbers ready for staff.
- [ ] Day 1 messages drafted for staff, customers and suppliers, and approved by the right owners.
- [ ] Readiness check run one or two days before completion, with any gaps recorded.

## B. Control (first hours)

- [ ] Administrator accounts for the directory, email, cloud, hosting, firewall and the main business system listed, and each owner named.
- [ ] Shared administrator passwords changed and stored in a managed vault.
- [ ] Domain registrations, DNS, certificates and cloud subscriptions confirmed as company-owned, with named administrators and recovery contacts.
- [ ] Any accounts held by individuals (founders, former staff, agencies) transferred or scheduled with a date.
- [ ] Break-glass accounts tested and sealed.
- [ ] Access for the seller's staff and former owners reviewed and reduced to what the transitional agreement needs.
- [ ] Leaver accounts from the last 12 months listed for review.

## C. Security (first day)

- [ ] MFA enforced on administrator accounts and on remote access.
- [ ] Monitoring of the target's alerts started, with a named person who receives them.
- [ ] Internet-facing services listed, and anything unnecessary (remote desktop, old portals, admin pages) closed or restricted.
- [ ] The target's network is not connected to the buyer's network yet, unless the agreed conditions are met.
- [ ] Incident contact route agreed: who calls whom, including the insurer's notification route.
- [ ] Endpoint protection coverage checked, with a list of unprotected devices.

## D. Continuity (first day)

- [ ] Payroll inputs, order entry, warehouse picking, dispatch, invoicing and banking confirmed working by the business owners.
- [ ] Email and phones working, with no domain or mailbox change.
- [ ] Backups for critical systems confirmed as having run, and a copy placed outside the target's server room.
- [ ] A restore test of the core system booked (within the first 30 days).
- [ ] Change freeze in place for non-essential changes, with a named approver for urgent ones.
- [ ] On-call cover for the first week defined, including out of hours for warehouse and operations.

## E. Support and communication (first day)

- [ ] One support number and mailbox published, with a person answering.
- [ ] Day 1 message sent to staff: what will not change, what will, who to ask.
- [ ] Customer and supplier messages sent as agreed, with account contact details unchanged unless planned.
- [ ] Target IT staff briefed on their role, the freeze and backfill support.
- [ ] A way for staff to report problems or questions, and a daily check of what came in.

## F. Finance and contracts (first week)

- [ ] One-off budget and approval route confirmed, with cost tracking started.
- [ ] Renewals and notices falling due in the first 100 days listed with decision dates.
- [ ] Licence use against entitlement checked for the main vendors.
- [ ] Supplier contacts and billing details confirmed, with no supplier left unpaid because of a change of ownership.

## G. End of Day 1 and first week

- [ ] Open issues from Day 1 listed with owners and dates.
- [ ] Daily check-in with the target IT lead and business owners for the first week.
- [ ] Risk and cost log updated from what Day 1 revealed.
- [ ] Inventory work started, to complete within the first 30 days.
- [ ] Short update to the steering group at the end of the first week.

## If something fails on Day 1

1. Tell the integration lead at once, with what is affected and since when.
2. Use the break-glass accounts and the saved backups before making further changes.
3. Fall back to the previous process where one exists (for example manual order entry).
4. Record the facts and timings. A clear log saves time later.
5. Hold a short review within a few days, and move the lessons into the plan.
